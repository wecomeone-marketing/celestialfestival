About

A Festival Named for the Stars

Celestial is a free open-air arts and music festival taking place across Laiki Geitonia — Larnaka's most characterful neighborhood — on June 13, 2026.

Seven stages, named after the seven sisters of the Pleiades constellation, fill the streets with reggae, jazz, tech-house, old-school electronica, art, and more from 6 pm. When the main stages close at 1:30 am, Taygeta opens for a ticketed after-party that runs through sunrise.

Free entry. Bring everyone.

Good to Know

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. The main festival from 6 pm to 1:30 am is completely free entry, all ages welcome. Only the after-party at Taygeta (1:30 am – 5 am) requires a wristband — €15 in advance / €20 at the door, available at Savino Rock Bar (Watkins 9, Larnaca · daily 2pm – 2am).
Is there parking?
Laiki Geitonia is in central Larnaka. Street parking is available in surrounding streets but limited on event night — we recommend walking, taxi, or rideshare. The neighborhood is fully walkable once you're there.
What about food and drinks?
All thirteen partner venues across the neighborhood will be serving food and drinks throughout the festival. With a Celestial wristband you get 5% off everything at every partner venue all night.
Is it family-friendly?
The main festival from 6 pm to 1:30 am is open to all ages. The Taygeta after-party from 1:30 am is 18+ only.
What if it rains?
June in Larnaka is consistently warm and dry. In the unlikely case of severe weather, we'll communicate any changes through our Instagram and Facebook stories — follow @celestial.festival.
Where do I get the after-party wristband?
Wristbands are sold in person at Savino Rock Bar (Watkins 9, Larnaca 6022), open daily from 2 pm to 2 am. €15 in advance, €20 at the door on event night.
